Privacy Notice

Olamide Olatayo Bello Corporation · Updated October 26, 2026

Who operates SprintForge

Olamide Olatayo Bello Corporation operates this SprintForge installation. This notice describes the data handled by the application as implemented.

Information stored

The application stores account names, email addresses, salted password hashes, organization memberships and roles, project and test records, comments, evidence URLs, audit entries and session information. Login-attempt controls temporarily store the connecting IP address. Behind a reverse proxy, that address may identify the proxy rather than the individual user. Hosting systems may also maintain operational logs.

How information is used

Information is processed to authenticate accounts, provide workspace features, enforce organization permissions, track test and release history, investigate errors and protect the service. Submitted project content is not sent to an AI provider by this version of SprintForge.

Who can access information

Organization members see workspace content according to their permissions. Platform administrators can manage account and tenant access, and authorized server operators can access stored data and backups to maintain the installation. External evidence destinations receive requests when you open their links; their policies apply to those visits.

Cookies and browser storage

An essential session cookie maintains your sign-in. The application stores your selected organization in local browser storage. This version does not embed advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.

Retention and requests

Workspace records remain stored until an authorized deletion or an operator retention action. Test executions and some audit records are retained by the application. Backups can retain earlier copies. Contact your organization administrator for referral to the Corporation’s platform administrator to request access, correction or deletion. Requests are assessed against applicable obligations and the integrity of shared organization records; automatic deletion is not currently available.

Security and updates

The service uses password hashing, session controls, organization access checks and HTTPS when properly deployed. No system can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to this notice will be published here with a revised date.
